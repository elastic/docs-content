* **Standalone {{agents}}**: In the [default processors feature flag](/reference/fleet/elastic-agent-standalone-feature-flags.md#elastic-agent-standalone-feature-flag-default-processors) to turn it off for all outputs, or in an [output's `default_processors` setting](/reference/fleet/elastic-agent-output-configuration.md#elastic-agent-output-default-processors) to turn it off only for the data sent to that output.
* **{{fleet}}-managed {{agents}}**: In the `default_processors` setting of an output's **Advanced YAML configuration**. Refer to the [{{es}}](/reference/fleet/es-output-settings.md#es-output-settings-yaml-config), [{{ls}}](/reference/fleet/ls-output-settings.md#ls-output-settings-yaml-config), or [Kafka](/reference/fleet/kafka-output-settings.md#kafka-output-settings-yaml-config) output settings.
