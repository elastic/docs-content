- Self-hosted: [](/deploy-manage/deploy/cloud-on-k8s.md) and [](/deploy-manage/deploy/cloud-enterprise.md)

To compare these options, refer to [Choosing your deployment type](/deploy-manage/deploy.md#choosing-your-deployment-type) and [](/deploy-manage/deploy/deployment-comparison.md).
