The interval that each data point on a chart represents depends on the length of the selected time period:

| Selected time period | Usage charts | Performance charts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More than 72 hours | 1 hour | 1 hour |
| More than 24 hours, up to 72 hours | 15 minutes | 15 minutes |
| More than 3 hours, up to 24 hours | 5 minutes | 5 minutes |
| 3 hours or less | 5 minutes | 1 minute |

Usage charts show resource consumption, such as VCUs. Performance charts show rate and latency trends.
