The [Elastic {{infer-cap}} Service](/explore-analyze/elastic-inference/eis.md) is charged based on the tokens used with its models, including embedding models, rerankers, and Elastic Managed LLMs. For embeddings and rerankers, usage is billed per million input tokens sent to the models. For LLMs, usage is billed per 1 million input tokens, output tokens, and cached tokens, depending on the model.
