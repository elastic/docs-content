# Explore your data in Discover

After a query returns results, **Discover** helps you understand them, from checking which fields your data has and reading the documents behind a value to finding patterns and changes over time.

* See [which fields your data has](explore-fields.md) and what they contain, and break the chart down by a field.
* Explore [individual results and documents](inspect-documents.md), and see the request that produced them.
* [Compare documents](compare-documents.md) side by side.
* [Sort your results by relevance](discover-search-for-relevance.md).
* [Inspect grouped results](inspect-grouped-stats.md), including groups of similar log messages ({{esql}} mode only).
* [Detect change points](detect-change-points.md) in a time series ({{esql}} mode only).
* [Analyze your data with AI](analyze-discover-data-with-ai.md) ({{esql}} mode only).
* Find patterns in your log data with [pattern analysis](run-pattern-analysis-discover.md) (classic mode only).
* Check [how the values of each field are distributed](show-field-statistics.md) (classic mode only).
* [Chart a field](visualize-field.md) from the fields list (classic mode only).
