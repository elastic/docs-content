Before you start, make sure you have data [indexed into {{es}}](/manage-data/ingest.md) or [install sample data](/manage-data/ingest/sample-data.md). The point-and-click editor uses [data views](/explore-analyze/find-and-organize/data-views.md) to access your {{es}} data. Data views are usually created automatically when you ingest data. You can also [create one manually](/explore-analyze/find-and-organize/data-views.md) to select the data that you want.
