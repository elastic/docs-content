# Manage {{alertzero}}

When [setting up {{alertzero}}](set-up-alertzero.md), you turn on the {{az-workers}} that you want, and they start with their default settings. To change how a {{az-worker}} behaves, or to check what it's doing, use the pages in this section.

## Where to start [manage-alertzero-where-to-start]

| Your goal | Start here |
|---|---|
| Turn a {{az-worker}} on or off, choose how much it can do without your approval, or change its other settings | [](configure-worker-settings.md) |
| Start a run yourself instead of waiting for the next scheduled run | [](run-worker-on-demand.md) |
| Check when a {{az-worker}} ran, what it did, and why a run failed | [](view-execution-history.md) |
