# Respond and contain

Triage the alerts that your detection rules generate, then act on the threats you confirm.

- [Triage detection alerts](/solutions/security/detect-and-alert/manage-detection-alerts.md): View, filter, and manage the alerts your rules generate.
- [Endpoint response actions](/solutions/security/endpoint-response-actions.md): Isolate hosts, run commands, and take other actions on endpoints, including through third-party EDR systems.

For tools that help you investigate an alert, refer to [Investigate](/solutions/security/investigate.md). To review alerts that AI has grouped into attacks, refer to [Attack Discovery](/solutions/security/ai/attack-discovery/index.md).
