# Hunt and assess posture

Review scores, findings, and benchmarks to find risks before they become incidents, and to check for drift since your last review.

- [Dashboards](/solutions/security/dashboards.md): Monitor alerts, posture, risk, and data quality at a glance.
- [Cloud Security](/solutions/security/cloud.md): Review posture findings, benchmarks, and vulnerabilities for your cloud accounts and Kubernetes clusters.
- [Entity analytics](/solutions/security/advanced-entity-analytics.md): Score risk for hosts, users, and services, and detect anomalous behavior.

For hands-on hunting tools such as Timeline, Discover, and {{esql}}, refer to [Investigate](/solutions/security/investigate.md).
