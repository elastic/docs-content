# Hunt for threats and assess security posture [hunt-and-assess-posture]

Use this section to look for threats proactively, before an alert points you to them, and to review how well your environment holds up against security best practices.

## Where to start [hunt-where-to-start]

| Your goal | Start here |
|---|---|
| Get an overview of alerts, detections, and data quality across your environment | [Dashboards](/solutions/security/dashboards.md) |
| Review cloud and Kubernetes configuration findings, cloud assets, and vulnerabilities | [Cloud Security](/solutions/security/cloud.md) |
| Assess the risk of hosts, users, and services, and find anomalous or privileged-user activity | [Entity analytics](/solutions/security/advanced-entity-analytics.md) |
| Hunt for threats with queries | [{{esql}} for security](/solutions/security/esql-for-security.md) |

To dig into a specific alert or event you find, refer to [Investigation tools](/solutions/security/investigate.md).
